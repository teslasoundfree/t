Use it in your Tesla

  1. Open this page in the Tesla browser while parked. Bookmark it so it is one tap away next time.
  2. Press Start engine and allow location access when asked. Without GPS the engine cannot rev: the sound follows your location / speed.
  3. Turn up the media volume. The engine follows your speed; accelerate harder for more revs.
  4. Stopped at a light? Hold Rev to blip the throttle.

Volume is always in view; the rest is under More settings, right below it. Intensity controls how eagerly acceleration turns into throttle and how late it shifts. Character goes from a stock, muffled engine to a lumpy cam with open pipes and crackle. Crackle sets how much the exhaust pops and the engine misfires; 0% is a clean-running engine.

Test drive lets you hear the car without moving: press the Gas and Brake pedals (lower on the pedal presses harder; the top of Gas holds your speed), or play the demo run.

Autoplay last engine starts the car you drove last as soon as the page opens. If the browser blocks sound until you touch the screen, tap anywhere to start it.

    Questions

    How do I make my Tesla sound like a gas car?

    While parked, open teslasound.io in the Tesla browser, press Start engine and allow location access. Turn up the media volume and drive: the engine sound follows your speed. Bookmark the page so it is one tap away next time.

    Does it play outside the car, like Boombox?

    No. TeslaSound plays inside the car through the regular speakers, like music. A web page cannot use the external Boombox speaker.

    Why does it need my location?

    The engine noise is driven by your speed, and the browser measures speed with GPS. Your location stays in the browser and is never sent anywhere.

    Is there a delay in the sound?

    A short one. In a Tesla the revs follow your speed about half a second behind, and when you floor it or lift off, the engine responds after about a second. About a quarter of a second of that is the car's own audio system; the rest is because GPS only sees the car speed up after it happens. On a phone, GPS updates once a second, so the sound reacts after about 1.5 to 2 seconds.

    Is it a recording of a real engine?

    No. Every sound is synthesized live by our own code, so the engine reacts to each change in speed instead of looping a sample.

    Does it work in other electric cars?

    Yes, in any car with a modern web browser and location access. You can also use it on a phone connected to the car speakers by Bluetooth. Keep the page open: the sound stops when the browser goes to the background.

    Is TeslaSound free?

    You get 5 minutes of engine time free every day. TeslaSound Pro costs $3 per month plus tax for unlimited driving on up to 2 cars, and you can cancel any time.

Stripe will email you a link to sign in, where you can cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, and you will not be charged again.

    Location is blocked

    TeslaSound needs your location to measure speed. The browser remembers "Never allow", so it has to be switched back on:

    Tesla browser

    1. Tap the lock or info icon next to the address, if one is shown, and set Location to Allow.
    2. Check the car's own privacy setting: Controls → Software → Data Sharing.
    3. Still blocked? The permission is stored per address, so open the site under a different address (for example with or without www.) and allow it when asked.

    Chrome / Edge

    1. Click the lock or sliders icon left of the address, set Location to Allow (or Reset permission).

    Safari

    1. Mac: Safari → Settings → Websites → Location. iPhone: tap aA → Website Settings → Location.

    Then reload the page and press Start engine again. Or use Test drive without GPS.
